# Laboratory Door Access

This system records card scans at a laboratory door and lets designated staff manage access or observe entry and exit.

## Language

**Admin**:
A laboratory administrator who creates and manages cards and their owners.

**Guard**:
A security officer who monitors who scanned in or out and when. A Guard does not manage cards or card owners.
_Avoid_: Employee (for the dashboard role)

**Card owner**:
A person assigned an RFID card to access the laboratory. A card owner does not need a dashboard account.

**Access event**:
A recorded card scan with its time, direction, and granted or denied result, including scans from unknown cards.
